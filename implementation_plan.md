# 🌟 "Best of the Best" Feature Expansion Plan

To make your Life RPG truly stand out and feel like a premium game, I propose adding two massive, highly visual, and deeply gamified features. 

## 1. ⚔️ THE ARENA (Weekly Epic Boss Battles)
We already have a hidden `bosses` table in your database! Let's bring it to life.
* **What it is:** A dedicated `/arena` page where you face off against a monstrous weekly boss (e.g., "The Procrastination Behemoth").
* **How it works:** When you complete quests in your real life, you don't just get XP—you **deal damage to the Boss**. The harder the quest, the heavier your strike.
* **The Visuals:** A massive, animated health bar, shaking screen effects when you land hits, and dramatic RPG battle music/UI.
* **The Reward:** Defeating the boss before the week ends grants a massive Gold and XP bounty.

## 2. 🔮 THE DESTINY ORACLE (Daily 3D Tarot Buffs)
A reason to open the app every single day.
* **What it is:** A mystical `/oracle` page where you draw your "Card of the Day".
* **How it works:** Beautiful 3D CSS card-flip animations. The card you draw gives you a real gameplay buff for that day. 
  * *Example:* Draw **The Chariot** → +20% XP for 24 hours.
  * *Example:* Draw **The Merchant** → +50% Gold for 24 hours.
* **The Vibe:** Mysterious, glowing particle effects, adding an element of luck and excitement to your daily tasks.
